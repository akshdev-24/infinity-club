# Infinity Club Jaipur — SEO Strategy

## Objective
Build durable organic visibility for Infinity Club in Jaipur, with priority around Mansarovar and Mansarovar Extension. The homepage targets the brand and the broad set of services already described on the website.

## Confirmed site identity
- Production domain: `https://infinityclubjaipur.in/` (the club's public website).
- Business name: Infinity Club.
- Address: Infinity Club, Muhana Mandi Road, near Kesar Circle, behind Eden Garden, Mansarovar Extension, Hajiwala, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302020, India.
- Phone: +91 89550 12924.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/infinityclub_official/

## Keyword focus
Primary: Infinity Club Jaipur.

Secondary themes are incorporated naturally in the page title, description, headings, visible facility/programme copy and contact information: gym in Mansarovar Jaipur; fitness centre in Jaipur; swimming pool/classes in Jaipur; cricket turf/box cricket in Jaipur; yoga and Zumba activities; martial arts classes.

## Page strategy
The supplied project is a single-page website. The homepage is the only indexable URL in the sitemap. Dedicated service URLs were intentionally not created: the current source does not contain enough distinct, verified service information to build eight substantial landing pages without repeating copy or creating doorway/thin pages. Expand only when the club supplies unique details, approved photographs, service-specific FAQs and accurate availability information.

## Trust and accuracy
- No aggregate rating or review schema is used.
- No invented prices, trainer credentials, pool measurements, age limits or class schedules are added.
- Weekly opening hours are marked up based on the hours published on the club's public website (Monday–Sunday, 06:00–22:00); the visible page still asks visitors to confirm before travelling.
- No coordinates are marked up because coordinates have not been independently verified.
- Only the supplied official Instagram profile is included in `sameAs`.

## Measurement
Track branded and local non-branded queries in Search Console. Compare impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query and landing page. Evaluate actual leads/calls separately from rankings. Rankings and indexing are not guaranteed.
