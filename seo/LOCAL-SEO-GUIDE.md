## Website implementation
The contact section and footer show the club's location and telephone. The LocalBusiness-compatible `SportsActivityLocation` entity in JSON-LD uses the same NAP details. Coordinates are deliberately omitted until the owner verifies the exact pin.

## Practical local visibility work
1. Ensure the Google Business Profile is claimed and owner-verified.
2. Match the public business name exactly; do not add keywords to the name unless they are genuinely part of the real-world name.
3. Select the closest accurate primary category from the categories actually offered in the GBP interface. Potential categories to check include Gym, Fitness center, Sports complex, Swimming pool, Sports club or Sports activity location. Availability and exact labels vary; choose only categories that accurately describe the venue.
4. Add secondary categories only for services genuinely offered at this location.
5. Verify the address pin and service area; do not create duplicate profiles for individual activities at one venue.
6. Add accurate opening hours and special holiday hours.
7. Add the official website and phone.
8. Upload current, authentic photographs: exterior/signage, gym floor, pool, cricket turf, class spaces and facilities. Obtain image rights and avoid stock images as evidence of the venue.
9. Publish occasional genuine updates about real events, service changes or seasonal availability.
10. Respond professionally to genuine reviews. Never buy reviews, gate review requests or ask staff to post fabricated feedback.

## Business-owner verification
Confirm business categories, weekly hours, current services, facility access and profile ownership directly in Google Business Profile. This guide does not claim that any suggested category is currently selectable in Google's category list.
